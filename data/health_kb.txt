Menstrual cycle has four phases: menstrual, follicular, ovulatory, luteal.
During the menstrual phase, gentle yoga and rest are recommended.
Iron-rich foods like spinach and lentils help replenish iron lost during menstruation.
PCOS symptoms include irregular periods, acne, excess hair growth, and weight changes.
Endometriosis causes severe pelvic pain during periods and requires medical diagnosis.
During the follicular phase, energy increases and cardio exercise is beneficial.
Ovulation typically occurs around day 14 of a 28-day cycle.
The luteal phase may bring PMS symptoms including mood changes, bloating, and fatigue.
Magnesium-rich foods like dark chocolate and nuts can help reduce PMS symptoms and cramps.
Tracking your cycle daily helps identify patterns and predict symptoms.
Staying hydrated and reducing caffeine can ease menstrual cramps.
Omega-3 fatty acids found in fish, flaxseed, and walnuts may reduce inflammation and period pain.
Heat therapy such as a warm compress or heating pad can relieve menstrual cramps.
A regular sleep schedule supports hormonal balance throughout the cycle.
Stress management techniques like deep breathing reduce cortisol and support cycle regularity.
Vitamin D deficiency is linked to more severe menstrual pain; get sunlight or consider supplements.
Light exercise during the luteal phase can improve mood and reduce bloating.
Symptoms that warrant seeing a gynecologist: periods lasting more than 7 days, very heavy bleeding, severe pain, or cycles shorter than 21 or longer than 35 days.
